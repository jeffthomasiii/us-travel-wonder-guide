# National Parks Coverage Tracker

National parks are **required coverage but not the organizing principle** of the United States Travel & Wonder Guide. This tracker exists to make sure every U.S. national park eventually appears somewhere in the guide while Phase 2 research continues to give equal editorial attention to state parks, forests, refuges, scenic roads, cultural places, islands, cities, geological features, and other travel-worthy destinations.

A checked park already has a live Wonder record. An unchecked park belongs in the Phase 2 candidate backlog and still needs editorial classification, source review, imagery, and planning metadata before publication.

## Phase 2 rule

Being on this tracker guarantees **eventual inclusion**, not a predetermined tier. A national park may be ★, ◆, or ○ depending on the area's editorial hierarchy. The same standard applies to non-park candidates: designation alone does not establish tier.
