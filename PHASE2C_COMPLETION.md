# Phase 2C Completion — National Baseline Production Pass

Phase 2C is complete as the guide's **first national production baseline**.

This does **not** mean the destination inventory is final or exhaustive. It means the content system has now moved beyond a proof-of-concept and every primary U.S. browse area has enough live content to exercise the same discovery, classification, and planning model.

## Completion baseline

The live guide now contains:

- **56 primary areas** — 50 states, Washington, D.C., Puerto Rico, U.S. Virgin Islands, Guam, Northern Mariana Islands, and American Samoa
- **120 Wonders total**
- **56 ★ Signature Wonders**
- **51 ◆ Worth Traveling For**
- **13 ○ Worth the Detour**
- at least **one non-Signature Wonder in every primary area**

Counts remain intentionally uneven beyond that baseline. Future additions are merit-driven, not quota-driven.

## Image policy at completion

The image policy has not been relaxed to finish the phase.

- the original 50 Signature Wonders have rights-verified imagery;
- the six expanded Signature Wonders have rights-verified imagery;
- the 12 Phase 1B mixed-tier Wonders have rights-verified imagery;
- the six Phase 2C Batch 1 Wonders have rights-verified imagery;
- later national expansion entries use the site's **neutral media fallback** until an individual asset passes the same rights-verification workflow.

No image should be promoted from fallback merely because it is visually convenient.

## Editorial meaning of completion

Phase 2C completion establishes a working national baseline, not a final list. The guide should continue to grow where research identifies destinations that genuinely qualify.

Future editorial work can therefore happen as normal content maintenance rather than as a prerequisite for proving the model.

National parks remain guaranteed eventual coverage, but they remain only one subset of the guide. The broader inventory should continue to include state and local lands, wildlife destinations, scenic roads, islands, architecture, historic and cultural landscapes, gardens, geology, coastal places, and other compelling destinations.

## Next phase

With the content model, national geography, search/filter system, mobile navigation, planning model, and national baseline now proven, the project can move into the dedicated **branding / design / layout phase** that was intentionally deferred during content development.
