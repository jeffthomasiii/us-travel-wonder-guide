How places qualify

A higher standard for a more meaningful guide.

★

Signature Wonders

The most exceptional places in each state, district, or territory. These are the destinations that define an area and give you a compelling reason to go.

◆

Worth Traveling For

Places that may not be the single defining landmark, but are well worth including in your travels. Memorable experiences with real depth and character.

○

Worth the Detour

Hidden gems, lesser-known places, or specialized interests that add richness to an area. They may not anchor an entire trip, but they are worth the extra effort if they fit your interests.

This isn’t a checklist.

It’s a more meaningful way to explore America.
