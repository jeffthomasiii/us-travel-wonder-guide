How the guide works

Three tiers. Three different reasons to go.

The tiers are not a ranking of beauty. They describe how strongly a destination can shape a trip.

★

Signature Wonder

The one destination that best makes the case for a state—the place you would show someone if you had one chance to demonstrate what makes that state extraordinary.

A state begins with one Signature Wonder, but that does not mean it has only one remarkable place.

◆

Worth Traveling For

A destination substantial enough to justify building part of a trip around it. It may not be the state's single defining wonder, but it deserves intentional travel time.

○

Worth the Detour

A place capable of transforming a route, road trip, or nearby stay into something much more memorable. These are the places you should know are there before you drive past them.

No quotas.

Some states may eventually have dozens of entries and others far fewer. The goal is usefulness, not numerical balance.
