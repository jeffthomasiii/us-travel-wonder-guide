# About the guide

The United States Travel & Wonder Guide starts with a simple premise: every U.S. state has somewhere capable of inspiring a trip, a detour, or a fresh look at the state itself.

The guide uses three editorial tiers rather than quotas:

- **★ Signature Wonder** — the one destination that best makes the case for the state.
- **◆ Worth Traveling For** — a destination significant enough to build part of a trip around.
- **○ Worth the Detour** — a place worth making time for when you are already nearby.

The proof of concept begins with one Signature Wonder in each state. Additional tiers, richer travel metadata, photography guidance, wildlife, RV notes, dog access, accessibility, nearby wonders and route-aware discovery can be added progressively.
